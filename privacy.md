## The short version

We do not set cookies. We do not run advertising or tracking pixels. We do not
sell or share your information. There are no accounts to create and nothing to
log in to. The only information you give us directly is what you type into the
contact form, and we only use it to reply to you.

## What we collect

### When you send us a message

Our contact form asks for your **name**, your **email address**, and your
**message**. You choose what to put in it. We use this only to read and reply
to your enquiry.

The form is handled by **Formspree**, a form-processing service based in the
United States, which passes the message on to us by email. Your message is
held by Formspree and in our email system.

### When you simply read the site

We use **GoatCounter** to understand roughly how many people visit and which
pages they read. It is a deliberately minimal analytics tool. It:

- sets **no cookies**;
- does **not store your IP address**, your full browser user-agent string, or
  any persistent identifier for you;
- records only aggregate figures, being your browser, operating system,
  country derived from your IP address, language, screen width, which pages
  were viewed, and the site you arrived from.

To recognise a returning visitor within a session it briefly holds a scrambled
combination of the site name, your IP address and your browser type in memory
for up to eight hours. This is never written to its records.

Our website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**. Like any web host, GitHub receives
and logs standard technical request information, including your IP address, in
order to deliver the page to you. We have no access to those logs.

### When you play a video

Our showreel section embeds players from **YouTube** and **Vimeo**. Nothing is
requested from either platform until you actively click to play a video. If
you read the page without playing anything, your browser never contacts them
and they cannot set anything on your device.

Once you do click play, that platform is providing the video and its own
privacy policy and cookies apply. We have no control over, and no visibility
of, what they collect.

## Cookies

**This website sets no cookies.** We use no advertising, remarketing, analytics
or preference cookies of our own, and no browser storage for tracking.

If you choose to play an embedded video, YouTube or Vimeo may set cookies at
that point. That is the only circumstance in which a cookie can be set through
this site, and it only happens if you ask for it.

## Fonts and other resources

The typefaces on this site are served from our own domain. We do not load
fonts, scripts or images from third-party content delivery networks, so simply
opening a page does not reveal your IP address to any company other than our
host and our analytics provider.
