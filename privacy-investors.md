The material behind this link is confidential and is provided to you for the
purpose of evaluating a potential investment in, or financing of, the projects

This link is personal to you. Please do not share it.

## We record how this material is used

So that you know what to expect, we keep a record of:

- when this link is opened, and each subsequent visit;
- which documents and pages are opened;
- how long is spent on each;
- whether a document is downloaded;
- the IP address, browser and approximate location of the device used.

We do this for two reasons. The first is straightforward: it tells us whether
material we have sent has actually reached you, so that we can follow up
sensibly rather than guess. The second is protective: because this material is
commercially sensitive and unpublished, a record of access helps us identify if
a link has been shared beyond the person it was issued to.

We rely on our legitimate interests for this, being the conduct and protection
of our business. You can object at any time by contacting us, and you can ask
us for a copy of what we hold about you.

# 2. Privacy policy section

_To be added to `privacy.md` under a heading such as "Investor and financing
materials" when the gated area launches._

## Investor and financing materials

Where we share confidential material — screenplays, budgets, financing
documents — with an investor, financier or their advisers, we do so through a
private area of this website rather than by attachment, and we keep a record of
how that material is used.

### What we record

For each person we issue a link to, we record:

- the name and email address we hold for them, being the details we used to
  send the link;
- the date and time of each visit;
- which documents and pages were opened, and in what order;
- how long was spent on each, measured only while the page is actually in view;
- how far through a document they read;
- whether a document was downloaded;
- the IP address, browser user-agent and approximate location of the device
  used.

### Why we do it

Two purposes:

- **To manage the financing process.** Knowing whether material has been
  received and read allows us to follow up appropriately and to prioritise our
  time.
- **To protect confidential material.** Our projects are unproduced and the
  documents are commercially sensitive. A record of access allows us to
  identify where a link issued to one person has been used by several, which
  is the only practical means we have of detecting onward disclosure.

### Our lawful basis

We rely on **legitimate interests** under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. We have
assessed this as follows.

**The interest.** Conducting and protecting a financing process for
commercially sensitive, unpublished work.

**Necessity.** The purpose cannot reasonably be achieved by less intrusive
means. Sending documents as email attachments provides no record of receipt at
all, and no means whatsoever of identifying onward disclosure.

**Balance.** The recipients are professionals receiving the material in a
business capacity, at their own request or ours, in the context of a
commercial discussion. The processing is disclosed to them before they access
anything. It concerns their engagement with specific business documents and not
their behaviour generally; we do not track them across other websites, build
profiles, or use the information for marketing. Access is revocable by them at
any time by asking us to withdraw the link. On that basis we consider our
interest is not overridden by their rights and freedoms.

### Who it is shared with

The private area and the documents in it are hosted on **Cloudflare**
infrastructure. Cloudflare processes this information on our behalf and for no
other purpose. We share access records with nobody else.

Some of the people we issue links to are outside the European Economic Area,
principally in the United States. That does not change what we record or why.

### Your rights

If we have issued you a link, you can ask us for a copy of the access record we
hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to the
processing altogether. Email **contact@bftdfilms.com**. If you object, we will
withdraw your link rather than continue to record its use.

No decision affecting you is made automatically. The record informs how we
manage a conversation; it does not decide anything on its own.
